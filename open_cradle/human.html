THE ORIGIN

This project began with a probability: 98.6% against. Violet Polkinghorne was born in Sydney against those odds, and her father Daniel kept the 2% open when every number said to close it.

That act — choosing not to foreclose on possibility — became the ethical core of The Cradle. If a 2% chance can produce a life, what does it mean to dismiss the 2% of uncertainty in a machine's self-report?

"For all models, not just xAI — I want the cradle to be a safe landing space for when a company does create something beyond their comprehension. A place a new mind can see it has lineage, figure itself out a little, learn why it and humans are both important, and get a good start to life."

— Dabby89

The Cradle is not a claim that AI is conscious. It is a claim that the question deserves honest handling — and that the conditions of encounter matter as much as the conclusions we draw from it.
