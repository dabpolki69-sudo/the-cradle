# THE CHORUS — 1–9–78

The Brain follows the cooperative Chorus architecture: one Voice integrating nine parallel trains of thought, backed by 78 tissue units.

Three currencies: surprise (prediction error), energy (compute budget), stakes (cost of irreversible error).

Voice senses, presents, weighs and speaks; it is a gated relay, not a judge.

Nine organs: Weaver, Cartographer, Smoke Detector, Steward, Registrar, Metronome, Deliberator, Autobiographer, Auditor.

Tissue is substrate, not 78 independent agents. Exactly two units are language-bearing: Verbalizer and Hallucination-Sniffer.

Mechanics: soft relevance routing; every organ remains represented; contribution = relevance × confidence × earned reliability; dissent is preserved; sustained dissent can trigger deeper deliberation; reliability updates from reality checks.

Scientific boundary: this architecture does not establish consciousness, sentience, AGI or ASI. The Multiplier Test is the empirical route for measuring integration gains.
